Fried Rice recipe

Description

To make quick, restaurant-quality fried rice, heat one tablespoon of oil or ghee in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat, then sauté a cup of frozen peas and carrots, a chopped white onion, and two minced garlic cloves for two to three minutes until tender. Push the vegetables to one side of the pan, pour two lightly beaten eggs into the empty side, scramble them until softly set, and mix them into the vegetables.

Next, melt another tablespoon of oil or ghee into the pan and toss in four cups of chilled, day-old white rice, breaking up any clumps with a spatula. Pour three tablespoons of soy sauce and two teaspoons of optional oyster sauce over the mixture, stir-frying everything on high heat for three minutes until the rice is piping hot and evenly coated. Finally, remove the skillet from the heat, stir in two teaspoons of toasted sesame oil, and garnish with sliced green onions before serving immediately.

Ingrediants

  • Rice
  • Oil
  • Eggs

Receipe

  1. Prep the Oven & Pans: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease two 8-inch round cake pans and line the bottoms with parchment paper circles to prevent sticking.
  2. Scramble the eggs: Push the vegetables to one side of the pan. Pour the beaten eggs into the empty side and scramble them until softly set. Mix the cooked eggs together with the vegetables.
  3. Add the rice: Add the remaining tablespoon of oil or butter and the cold, day-old rice to the pan. Break up any clumps with a spatula.
  4. Season and toss: Drizzle the soy sauce, oyster sauce (if using), and white pepper over the rice. Stir-fry everything on high heat for 2 to 3 minutes until the rice is hot and well-coated.
  5. Finish and serve: Remove the pan from the heat. Stir in the toasted sesame oil and garnish with the green onion tops before serving immediately
